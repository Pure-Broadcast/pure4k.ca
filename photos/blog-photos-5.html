My Old School Photography Background

I got into Photography when film was the only option, so I learned how to process film in my own darkroom at one time. My video rigs have all been built around a DSLR camera, specifically Nikon, because I LOVE the cinematic looks I can achieve.

In the industry, I've worked with broadcast cameras which offer many different options but at massive expense, my cameras are pro/sumer which means I can make my mirrorless camera do what most other very expensive cameras can do, affordably.

All photos are processed but zero Photoshop, color processing only. I'd love to talk to you about your next project.
