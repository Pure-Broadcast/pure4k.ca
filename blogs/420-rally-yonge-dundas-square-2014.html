420 Rally Yonge Dundas Square 2014

Life Before Canada Legalized Cannabis

A Positively Influencial Video

This documentary styled video is of a very strong movement towards the legalization of Cannabis in Canada, and the purpose of the video was to show the positivity of this movement by hearing from the people themselves.

1/19

A Short Version Released That Day

Thousands Of Views Worldwide Immediately

At the time, the entire world was watching Canada peacefully protest it's government to legalize Cannabis and held 420 Rallys for years. Since I had documented many of them, I knew everyone involved and got this stage view of the crowd hotboxing Yonge Dundas Square from the perfect angle.

This was the most popular 420 Rally to date AND potentially swayed the vote towards approving recreational use as it happened shortly after this event.
